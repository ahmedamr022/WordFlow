# WordFlow
# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Product Vision

WordFlow is an AI-powered English learning platform that teaches users naturally through reading, listening, typing, spaced repetition, and personalized AI explanations.

Unlike traditional apps that rely on memorization, WordFlow focuses on language acquisition through repeated exposure and active interaction.

The goal is to make learning English feel like reading enjoyable stories rather than studying a textbook.
